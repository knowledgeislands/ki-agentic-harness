# Zettelkasten

The [Zettelkasten][zettelkasten-ref], or slip box, is Niklas Luhmann's note-taking method: each note holds one idea, carries a stable identifier, and is densely linked to other notes so that knowledge grows through its connections.

It is a source of the atomic-card and linking practice behind [Contextual Atomicity][atomicity] and [Semantic Markdown][semantic-markdown], where each card is one coherent, addressable resource linked to others.

Zettelkasten-style graphs often add a [Map of Content][moc], a note that serves as an entry point to the cards on one theme without holding their knowledge itself.
