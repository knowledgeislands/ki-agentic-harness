# Knowledge-graph cards from the Inbox

For ki.reg.guilds. These are the Inbox's staged knowledge-graph cards, the concept, method and tooling cards it curated under `knowledge/curated/knowledge-islands/ki-principal/Pillars/knowledge-graph/`, all still `status: proposal` or `future-concept`. personal.reg.inbox-terra delivered them here on 7 November 2026 at Kris's word, as material for the knowledge-graph method skill in [d12-kg.design.md §2](../../_WORK/ki.reg.guilds/skills/d12-kg.design.md), proposed as `ki-knowledge-graph`. The delivery is a one-off exception to the Inbox's dispatch hold, for these 22 cards only.

- **Possibly a keystone skill.** Kris suggests the skill may be a keystone skill, foundational and used across many skills, like those in [`skills/keystone/`](../../../skills/keystone/). Guilds decides how to structure the skill from the cards.
- **Long-term home.** The cards' long-term home as knowledge may still be Knowledge Islands Principal's Pillars, the path they were staged under. That is Guilds' and ki.sop.main's call, not the Inbox's.
- **Shadow-node life cycle.** [`layered-graphs.md`](./layered-graphs.md) now carries the shadow-node life cycle Kris proposed on 7 November 2026: lift, prune, touch point, look up before you grow, and regrow. A skill could enforce it, in particular looking up before growing a new node.
- **Shadows left behind.** The Inbox keeps a shadow node at each card's old path, pointing here, so its existing links still resolve.
- **Copied as they were.** The cards are byte for byte as staged. Their links within this folder resolve; links that leave it, such as `../../../tools-kig/tools-kig.md`, are relative to the Inbox's staging tree and do not resolve here.
