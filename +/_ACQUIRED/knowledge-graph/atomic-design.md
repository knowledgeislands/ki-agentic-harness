# Atomic Design

[Atomic Design][atomic-design-book] is Brad Frost's method for designing interfaces by composition: atoms combine into molecules, molecules into organisms, and organisms into templates and pages.

It is a loose analogy for the atomic knowledge behind [Contextual Atomicity][atomicity], not a hierarchy Knowledge Islands imposes.
