# Monthly mechanical governance review

## Goal

Regularly identify evidence-backed opportunities to reduce repeated mechanical governance cost without hiding judgment or widening write authority.

## Procedure

1. Re-ground the Harness and estate evidence for repeated checks, generated publications, transcript grounding, and recurring inventory work.
2. Record the repeated operation, evidence source, frequency signal, owner, deterministic input, cost, and judgment or safety boundary.
3. Classify each result as retained judgment, a better diagnostic or prepared context, a bounded mechanical check, or a separately owned proposal.
4. Route only non-trivial changes to a receiver-owned roadmap item; do not change skills, configuration, or runtime state as part of the review.

## Successful-run evidence

The linked run records every measured candidate, its classification, proposed owner, testable no-write failure mode, and any separately created follow-up. It explicitly excludes scores, automatic external writes, and compressed evidence that hides uncertainty.

## Obsolescence

Delete this template only when another accepted, repository-wide evidence mechanism supersedes this recurring review without reducing its safety or judgment boundary.
