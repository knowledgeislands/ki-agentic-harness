# Monthly decision reconciliation

## Goal

Keep the estate's current Decision Record surface coherent while retaining historical reasoning as evidence rather than rewriting it.

## Procedure

1. Inventory canonical Decision Record collections and each record's identity, scope, applicability, status, stated predecessor or successor, and related current contract.
2. Classify roadmap items, trades, generated publications, and unmarked historical notes as evidence outside the rewrite scope.
3. Review potentially overlapping or superseded current decisions and propose retention, explicit links, in-place revision, or separately approved prune selection.
4. Route every content change, consolidation, or collection cleanup to its owning repository; do not rewrite, delete, or renumber a Decision Record in this review.

## Successful-run evidence

The linked run reconciles inventory totals with inspected collections and records each proposed disposition, preserved reasoning relationship, and receiving owner. It makes clear that historical roadmap and trade artifacts were neither rewritten nor treated as current authority.

## Obsolescence

Delete this template only when an accepted replacement can reliably keep current decision authority discoverable while preserving the same historical-evidence boundary.
