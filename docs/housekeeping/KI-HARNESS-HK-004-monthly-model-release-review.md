# Monthly model release review

## Goal

Periodically reassess model and executable-route evidence deeply enough to support reviewed recommendation, support, and retirement movements without changing consumer defaults directly.

## Procedure

1. Review every current model, route, benchmark, and unresolved weekly signal against the registered primary and independent sources, including material releases since the previous run.
2. Preserve each evaluated unit and benchmark applicability; do not numerically merge bare-model, provider-endpoint, model-agent, or complete-environment results without an explicit defensible method.
3. Propose recommendation-ring, support-state, retirement-state, or benchmark-lifecycle movements only when the applicable evidence gates are met, retaining uncertainty and material counter-evidence.
4. Update the committed snapshot only through the reviewed run, and route every approved trial, adapter, installation, default, or retirement consequence to its owning repository through `ki-next`.

## Successful-run evidence

The linked run identifies the reviewed models, routes, benchmarks, sources, local-fit evidence, unchanged or proposed movements, counter-evidence, and owner-local follow-ups. It distinguishes radar advice from consumer configuration and records an explicit no-change result when no movement is justified.

## Obsolescence

Delete this template only when another accepted mechanism provides equivalent periodic model-route reassessment, evidence retention, movement review, and receiver-owned implementation routing.
