# KI-HARNESS-GOV-226: Banner Rendered Glyphs

## Goal

The `ki-coordination` standard's [Banner section](../../skills/change-management/ki-coordination/references/standards-coordination.md#banner) keeps the emoji as the work model's written notation and admits a rendered form: a surface may draw the five states with the line glyphs from `@knowledgeislands/icons`, always with the state's words beside them.

## Context

Kris Brown decided BANNER-GLYPHS a) on 20 October 2026 in the Observatory work, under the overnight authority recorded as OVERNIGHT in the [Observatory decisions log](/Users/krisbrown/workspaces/knowledge-islands/ki-principal/+/_WORK/ki.observatory/decisions.md): keep the emoji as the standard's written notation and let a rendered surface draw the five line glyphs with the state's words beside them. The recommendation was taken at high confidence.

The Banner writes each state with emoji: the worker, the supervisor, a named target, on hold and finished. The Knowledge Islands design says no emoji on rendered surfaces, and the Observatory's Work tab prints the Banner unchanged because Kris chose one notation everywhere ([PDR-KI-OBSERVA-008](https://github.com/knowledgeislands/apps-observatory.local/blob/main/docs/decisions/PDR-KI-OBSERVA-008-line-glyphs-beside-tabs-and-subjects.md)), which is the design's one "no emoji" exception.

ki-design asked the question and drew five single-colour candidates in its house style, marked proposed and passing `lintIcon`: worker (a mallet), supervisor (head and shoulders, no face), named target (a map pin), on hold (an anchor) and finished (a pennant on a staff). They are in [`docs/briefs/banner-glyph-candidates.md`](https://github.com/knowledgeislands/ki-design/blob/main/docs/briefs/banner-glyph-candidates.md) and [`docs/briefs/banner-glyphs/`](https://github.com/knowledgeislands/ki-design/tree/main/docs/briefs/banner-glyphs), and ki-design's hand-off text is section 2 of [`-/observatory-handoff/ANSWER-2026-10-19.md`](https://github.com/knowledgeislands/ki-design/blob/main/-/observatory-handoff/ANSWER-2026-10-19.md). The route it set is that the standard changes first and the Work tab follows.

## Boundary

- In: a Banner-section statement that the emoji stay the written notation in reports, terminals and commits, and that a rendered surface may draw the five line glyphs from `@knowledgeislands/icons` in their place, always with the state's words beside them; the matching line in the work model wherever the harness restates the Banner notation.
- Out: drawing or publishing the glyphs, which ki-design owns; the Work tab, which apps-observatory.local owns; any change to the written notation, the state words or the banner's shape.
