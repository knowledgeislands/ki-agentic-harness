# KI-HARNESS-GOV-216: Three-Part Coordinator Updates

## Goal

Every coordinator's timed update under `ki-coordination` says three things: what is done, what is left on the plan, and what is outstanding, in place of today's one or two lines; the hook line and the output style that carry the update rule are regenerated from the standard.

## Context

On 18 October 2026 Kris Brown asked, in the ways-of-working work, that every update say what is done, what is left on the plan and what is outstanding; that work's coordinator applies it already, but the [Coordinator responsiveness](../../skills/change-management/ki-coordination/references/standards-coordination.md#coordinator-responsiveness) cadence still asks for "timed updates of one or two lines". Kris then answered "yes, go ahead with all the recommended" in the ways-of-working decisions log, "owner answers, batches 6 and 7", read back as taken: "UPDATE-SHAPE a) - adopt three-part coordinator updates in ki-coordination at the report-shape review around 21 October, regenerating hook line and output style."

## Boundary

- In: the update cadence and heartbeat wording in `skills/change-management/ki-coordination/references/standards-coordination.md`, and the `hook-line` and `output-style` sources in its Generated surfaces, regenerated through `ki dev skill generate ki-coordination --write` into `hooks/coordinator-reminder.sh` and `output-styles/ki-coordinator.md`; any `ki-coordination` judgment item that quotes the cadence.
- Out: the owner report, the Summary and the banner, whose shape is unchanged; background agents' status lines, which `ki-delegation` owns; building any of it before the report-shape review.

## Shaping

Scheduled for the report-shape review around 21 October 2026, as Kris set it: promote it to Next, plan and build it then, not before. No record in this repository blocks it. The change is one cadence paragraph and one heartbeat paragraph in the standard, the matching clause of the hook line's third hard rule and of the output style's turn paragraph, and a regeneration.

## Discussion

Captured and adopted to Soon by the `harness-followups` run on Kris Brown's UPDATE-SHAPE a) answer, which set the timing; the run's brief said not to build it now. Open for planning: whether the three parts are three short lines or one line in three clauses, and whether the heartbeat's "each running agent's current step" folds into "left on the plan".
