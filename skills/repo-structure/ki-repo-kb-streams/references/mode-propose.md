# Mode PROPOSE — route new KB work

_On-demand procedure for Streams PROPOSE. The shared model lives in [`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md) and is already loaded; this file is the procedure only._

1. Classify the request: substantive prospective forward work belongs in `Streams/Roadmap/`; a recurring obligation belongs in the configured Activity collection governed by `ki-repo-kb-activities`; a trade is out of scope until a KB trade placement is explicitly adopted.
2. For roadmap work, use `ki-work-roadmap` to reload the flat records and `Streams/Roadmap/_ISSUES.md`, then deduplicate against the canonical queue. If an existing record covers the same outcome, report that record and do not allocate another identity.
3. Otherwise select the configured issuing area and allocate its next serial from `_ISSUES.md`. Commit that ledger advance on its own, in the base's designated roadmap writing checkout, before the record exists; the [roadmap standard](../../../change-management/ki-work-roadmap/references/standards-repository-roadmaps.md#number-reservation) owns the ordering. Then create the flat roadmap item as `status: triage` with no horizon. Work that fails the graduation test stays an idea in its Project note's `## Notes` section, or in `_IDEAS.md` when it has no Project. Report its title, path, and ID after capture; capture itself does not adopt or prioritise the work.
4. Require explicit human approval before moving a record out of triage, renaming it, or cancelling it. Apply an approved adoption through `ki-next`. Route an approved cancellation to `ki-accept`, which records `status: cancelled` with its resolution before any later prune. Never delete intake directly or infer approval from silence or surrounding discussion.
5. For a recurring obligation, hand off to `ki-repo-kb-activities` and the `ki-work-housekeeping` profile. Do not create a separate Streams definition or a due run until the owning procedure authorises it.
