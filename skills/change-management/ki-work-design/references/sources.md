## Last review

On 2026-10-07 the run's brief, three reviews, merged report and decisions file shaped the stage contracts and templates. The run first kept its artefacts only in local state, and then beside its Decision Record in the decisions collection. The owner then ruled that design artefacts are temporary working material kept beside the subject Project or Initiative and deleted once consolidated, and that the decisions collection holds only Decision Records and their index; the artefact-home rule follows that ruling.

Kris's 14 October 2026 decision-tag rule, carried by the [coordination decisions life cycle](../../ki-coordination/references/standards-decisions.md#decisions-life-cycle), supersedes numbered decision entries. Reports and the owner's decisions use matching tags; numbered reflection points remain claims.

On 2026-11-08 Kris took DOC-TYPES a and STD-BASE a at 22:05 CET, relayed by `personal.reg.inbox`: two document types only, the change document (`.change.md`) and the standard, a design note becoming a change document; and every change document carries `Base: ki-agentic-harness@<commit>` and a `Touches:` line, rebased by reading `git diff <base>..main -- <touched files>`. The [artefact home](standards-work-design.md#artefact-home) now names the change document; `ki-authoring` owns the type and its header.
