# Coordinating-work standard: listening and host sync

This file holds the inbox watch, the two thread capabilities and host sync. It extends [the core](standards-coordination.md).

## Inbox watch

- **Inbox watch:** a coordinating thread listens for its messages only when the owner asks it to. Kris asked under INBOX-WATCH on 31 October 2026 for an open thread to keep "periodically checking to see if you've got any new messages and acting on those", made it the rule the same day under LISTEN-STATUS, and narrowed it to an opt-in under LISTEN-OPT-IN: "I think fundamentally any of the coordination [threads] can be set up in this mode, but we've got to expressly ask them to be. And then secondarily ... the personal.reg.rig-<hostname> ones, which run on a regular basis doing this". So a coordinating thread listens once the owner expressly asks it to, in words such as "listen for messages" or "keep listening", and the coordinator records that request in the work's decisions log; a Rig host sync thread, `personal.reg.rig-<host>`, listens by its role without being asked, because [host sync](#thread-capabilities) implies listening. While a listening thread is open and its work is not draining, On hold or Finished, the coordinator keeps one inbox watcher armed, whoever holds the work; work [Snoozing](standards-holds.md#putting-work-on-hold) keeps it armed On hold too. "stop the wake" or "stop listening" ends listening until the owner asks again, and a thread never asked does not listen. The heartbeat while runs go is separate and stays, listening or not, and joins the watcher in one loop. Kris set the watcher's shape under QUIET-LISTEN on 31 October 2026: "for a thread which is listening ... we don't need it to give an update unless it actually gets new information that's landed". The watcher is one long-running background loop, a detached shell loop that spends no model time: every couple of minutes it fetches and fast-forwards local `main` silently and compares the Git object of the work's `messages.md` with the one it started from, reading no content, and it exits only on news, a new line in `messages.md` or a run in this thread's group finishing; a pull that cannot fast-forward also ends it, as news needing a resolve. It has no timed bound while idle, so a quiet period produces no wake, no tool calls and no chat line. On a wake the thread speaks only about what landed: on a new line the coordinator acts on it under [Messages between work](standards-messages.md#messages-between-work), commits, re-arms and tells the owner in one timed line what it did, and on a finished run it receives the run in one timed line and re-arms. A thread never holds more than one loop; after a compaction or a recovery it checks whether one is armed before arming another. The watcher lives and dies with the session: it is a session wake under WAKE-SESSION, not a scheduled agent, and promises nothing once the thread closes. A thread whose runtime has no proven way for the watcher's exit to wake it checks its inbox only at owner messages and run events, and shows itself as not listening. A shared shell command for the watcher and each runtime's wake are still open questions for the owner.
- **Listening:** a banner whose thread has the listening capability, `listening_enabled: true` in its `state.md`, shows `👂 listening` under [Banner](standards-coordination.md#banner)'s mode lines while the watcher is armed, `listening_status: on`, and `🔇 not listening` otherwise, `listening_status: off`: while draining, from `pause`, `done for now` or `drain` until the drain ends, because a drain stops listening, On hold and paused, while work [Snoozing](standards-holds.md#putting-work-on-hold), the worker's idle hold included, keeps showing `👂 listening`, after "stop the wake" or "stop listening", or where the runtime cannot be woken. A thread without the capability shows no listening line at all.

## Thread capabilities

A coordination thread may have two capabilities, as Kris set under THREAD-CAPABILITIES on 31 October 2026: "we've got coordination threads, and then I think we've got syncing threads. A coordination thread can be a syncing thread which syncs between hosts or a host sync capability. And then I think we've got one which is like a listening capability". Each is switched on only by the owner's express word, recorded in the work's decisions log, or by an agreed role, such as a Rig host sync thread's.

Each thread records its capabilities in four `state.md` fields under the `ki-work-package` [state file](../../../governance/ki-work-package/references/standards-work-package.md#state-file), as Kris set under FOOTER-CAPABILITY-STATUS-2, relayed on 1 November 2026: `listening_enabled` and `syncing_enabled`, `true` or `false`, say whether the thread has the capability, and `listening_status` and `syncing_status`, `on` or `off`, say whether it is running now. If `syncing_enabled` is true, `listening_enabled` must be true, because a syncing thread is always a listening thread. The coordinator sets `_enabled` when the owner's word or the agreed role grants the capability and clears it only when the owner withdraws it; it sets `_status` at every arm, stop, drain, hold and resume. "stop the wake" or "stop listening" turns the status off and leaves the capability enabled. At [Finished](standards-mark.md#finishing-project-work) the close-out clears both `_enabled` fields.

- **Listening:** the thread keeps the [inbox watcher](standards-coordination.md#coordinator-responsiveness) armed, under LISTEN-OPT-IN. A listening thread also tends its own work package, as Kris set under LISTEN-TENDS-PACKAGE the same day: "if a thread is listening, it's important that it is also helping to manage and refine the work packages and make sure they're in good state". At a wake it makes at most one small tidy, within its turn budget: it keeps `state.md`'s front matter and next steps true; prunes received reports and spent briefs under the `ki-work-package` [tidy rules](../../../governance/ki-work-package/references/standards-work-package.md#tidying-the-work-folder); strikes a decision, or reduces it to a one-line pointer, once a durable home carries it, under the [decisions life cycle](standards-decisions.md#decisions-life-cycle); keeps `discussions.md` free of answered entries; and raises what it cannot settle as a query. It says nothing about a tidy unless something needs the owner. It never edits another work's package, though it may point out a problem in one with a line in that work's `messages.md`.
- **Host sync:** the thread is its Mac's sync thread. It repeats each pass until the Mac and GitHub are clean and level, or until something needs judgement, and only then waits for its next slot, as Kris set under SYNC-UNTIL-CLEAN the same day: "a resync doesn't finish until it's got to a point of being clean". It stays quiet on quiet passes, under SYNC-QUIET: nothing when nothing changed, at most one short line when something did, and a full report only for something that needs judgement or the owner. Host sync implies listening.

The banner's [mode lines](standards-coordination.md#banner) show the listening capability's status, `👂 listening` or `🔇 not listening`. Host sync has no banner line, as Kris decided under SYNC-LINE-OFF at about 18:00 GMT on 4 November 2026: remove the sync mode line from the banner entirely, now, because Agora keeps the machines in sync through each machine's household work package, so the line no longer says anything true and useful. The `syncing_enabled` and `syncing_status` fields stay in `state.md`.
