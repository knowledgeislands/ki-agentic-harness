# Coordinating-work standard: holds

This file holds draining, the paused hold, Snoozing and release. It extends [the core](standards-coordination.md).

## Putting work on hold

Read IMPORTANT messages first, cancel every wake held by this session, the inbox watcher included, and report whether anything remains running, queued or scheduled. Work going [Snoozing](#putting-work-on-hold) re-arms the inbox watcher once the snooze lands.

On hold is the state the note's On hold paragraph sets, with its two reasons, paused and more expected, which this standard calls Snoozing, and drain is the activity into it, never a state. A hold is agreed only from With supervisor: the owner says `pause` or `done for now`, and a coordinator may propose one in its send. The one exception is the idle hold below, which the worker sets itself. A hold serves a move between hosts, closes work for a period, or rests work between waves, and never says which host the work comes back up on. A conversational session's ad hoc work goes On hold through a state-file update, with no hold report. Kris set the drain on 2026-10-15, in these words:

> 1. Check you are wrapped up. Nothing is running or queued: let any agent that will finish within about 15 minutes finish; otherwise stop it at a safe point and record where it stopped and how to restart it. Do what you still can to finish small open items yourself, through a run if needed. Do not start new work.
> 2. Commit and push. In every repository you or your agents touched, commit your own work by explicit path under its gate, pull with rebase, and push fast-forward only. Never use --no-verify or force-push. Name any other session's commit or uncommitted file; don't touch it.
> 3. Park everything in the checkpoint. Strike a mark. Move every open query (tag, one-line context, recommended option), held item, parked item, waiting-on item and the next step into the checkpoint, set running to nothing and state to drained, and copy into the thread folder anything from ~/.local/state/ki/agents/<run>/ needed to resume. Remove re-copied retired reports by explicit path. Commit and push the checkpoint and thread folder.
> 4. Do not make a baseline or empty commit; Kris does that afterwards.
> 5. Give a short drain report, and nothing else: [...]
>
> If you cannot drain (unpushable work or a run that must not be stopped), use the BUSY frame instead and say in one line what is blocking.

Applied under the work model, the checkpoint being the work package and the thread folder the work folder:

- **Wrapping up:** an agent stopped at a safe point has where it stopped and how to restart it recorded in `state.md`'s next steps, with its brief kept open in its focus folder or `briefs/<run>.md`, and no new work starts once the drain begins. Every settled Orca worker the work started is reused, retained or released under [Closing out Orca workers](standards-mark.md#closing-out-orca-workers).
- **Committing:** each repository's own gate runs as usual under `ki-git`; the pull is fast-forward only into a clean checkout, under [Committing the work folder](standards-coordination.md#committing-the-work-folder), and pushing follows [Pushing beneath other sessions](standards-sync.md#pushing-beneath-other-sessions), so stacked commits are pushed with the coordinator's own and omitted from the report. Another session's uncommitted file is named in `## Left over` and left alone. A hold report keeps nothing about pushes beyond a failure.
- **Tidy first:** before any hold, paused, Snoozing or idle, the folder keeps nothing already dealt with, under the `ki-work-package` [tidy before a hold](../../../governance/ki-work-package/references/standards-work-package.md#tidying-the-work-folder); the rubric fails a held folder that still does.
- **Parking:** the mark is struck under [The mark](standards-mark.md#the-mark), decisions are struck under the [decisions life cycle](standards-decisions.md#decisions-life-cycle) and the folder pruned. The coordinator moves every open query from `## Open` to `## Parked` in `discussions.md` with its Needs tag, one line of context and its recommended option, writes `state.md`'s next steps for when the work is back in use, and sets `state.md`'s front matter to `state: on-hold` with `hold.reason`, `paused`, or `more-expected` for Snoozing, `hold.release` in words and `since`. Then it commits with the subject `docs(work): <id> hold, paused` or `docs(work): <id> hold, snoozing`, and pushes. It makes no baseline or empty commit.
- **Report:** the hold report is the send that announces the hold. It opens with the [report heading](standards-coordination.md#banner), its form line reading `**⏸️ On hold · Hold report at <local time> · mark <local time>**`, or `**💤 Snoozing (on hold) · Hold report at <local time> · mark <local time>**` while Snoozing, then `## Done`, `## Parked` and `## Left over`, and nothing else: no `## In Progress`, no `## Queries` and no **Context**, **Query** or **Options**. `## Done` carries one line per repository. It closes with the On hold or Snoozing banner, which lists nothing waiting under [Banner](standards-coordination.md#banner): its counts read `⏸️ drained · 🅿️ <n> queries parked`, or `💤 waiting for a message · 🅿️ <n> queries parked` while Snoozing, and its items are only `[parked]` and `[release]`. Its closing thread line is the last line of the report; no send or resume prompt follows it.
- **Cannot drain:** work with unpushable changes or a run that must not be stopped stays With worker, blocked; its report says in one line what is blocking and closes with its thread line.
- **Drain without hold:** `drain` alone stops launching while the work stays With worker; when everything has finished the coordinator reports, its `## Done` carrying `DRAINED`, or `NOT DRAINED` with what remains. Any explicit drain check, such as "are we drained?", puts its result in `## Done` the same way, and the thread line stays the last line of the report in every case.
- **Territory hold:** the owner issues it through territory work. Every piece of Project work goes on hold first and territory work last. Territory work's `state.md` next steps name the work that restarts when the hold lifts and the work that stays on hold.
- **Snoozing:** a hold that waits for more to come is Snoozing, `💤 Snoozing (on hold)`, the reason the Work Process note calls more expected, and it replaces both the more-expected hold and the listening hold. The owner asks for it in words such as `snooze`, "hold on snooze waiting for a message" or "done for now, more to come"; regular work rests Snoozing between occurrences, and a Project may rest Snoozing between waves. The coordinator records the request in the decisions log and drains as above; once the snooze lands it re-arms the one inbox watcher, setting `listening_enabled: true` where the owner asked it to wait for a message and it was not already, and `listening_status: on`. `hold.release` reads `a message arrives, or resume`. The banner follows the [one table of states](standards-coordination.md#banner): heading `### 💤 Snoozing (on hold) · at <time>`, counts `💤 waiting for a message · 🅿️ <n> queries parked`, never `drained`, because the thread still listens, items `[parked]` and ``[release] a message arrives, or `resume` ``, then `👂 listening`. A new line in `messages.md`, or new work arriving, releases it under **Release** below and the coordinator acts on it; "stop the wake" or "stop listening" sets `listening_status: off` and shows `🔇 not listening`, the work staying Snoozing until `resume` or a message its territory's State of play handles under [acting for idle work](standards-territory.md#territory-work). Once the session closes, the State of play acts for the snoozing package on its messages alone.
- **Idle:** a worker may put its own work into Snoozing by itself, the idle hold, and into no other hold. It may go idle only while all five hold: no run running or queued; nothing in handoff to a target; no unprocessed line in `messages.md`; no open query with the owner in `discussions.md`, parked queries standing; and every repository the work touched committed and pushed. Any query with the owner keeps the work With supervisor. Its folder is tidied first, as for any hold, but idle needs no drain and no hold report: the coordinator sets `state: on-hold` with `hold.reason: more-expected`, `hold.set_by: worker`, `hold.release: a message arrives, or resume` and `since`, commits `docs(work): <id> hold, idle` and says so in one line. Its banner is Snoozing's, its heading `### 💤 Snoozing (on hold) · idle · at <time>`. It listens only while `listening_enabled` is true. A line arriving on a listening idle thread is received by the worker at once, moving the work straight to With worker rather than Handoff to worker, because the worker is already there. The owner's `pause`, `done for now` or `snooze` turns the idle hold into the owner's form, `hold.set_by: owner`; a hold with no `set_by` was set by the owner.
- **Paused work and important lines:** a paused hold stays closed to every line, `URGENT` and `IMPORTANT` included. The line waits unread; its territory's State of play, acting for the held work under [acting for idle work](standards-territory.md#territory-work), shows the count and puts an important or urgent line to the owner as a `resume` query in its own `discussions.md`, the package staying paused until the owner answers; a sender with genuinely urgent work sends it to a live owner under [Urgent work](standards-messages.md#messages-between-work) rather than into the paused queue.
- **Release:** `resume`, the release condition being met, or, while Snoozing, a new message or new work arriving, moves the work to Handoff to worker; the coordinator re-reads the work package, moves the parked queries back to `## Open` in `discussions.md` and starts from `state.md`'s next steps. `finish` moves it to Finished. A paused hold older than 31 days is flagged in the views.
