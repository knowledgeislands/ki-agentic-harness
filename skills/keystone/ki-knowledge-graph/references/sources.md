## Last review

On 7 November 2026, under the decisions above and an away-window grant to build the skills only, the skill was written from the guild's design note, the 22 knowledge-graph cards, the funnel and curation cards, and the `ki-acquire`, `ki-repo-kb` and `ki-skills` standards.
