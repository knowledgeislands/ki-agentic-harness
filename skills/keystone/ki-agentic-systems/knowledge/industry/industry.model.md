# Model

A model is what decides: the one thing every other layer of an agentic system is built from.

A model, in agentic systems a large language model (LLM), takes text and images in and gives text out. It acts on nothing by itself: it reads no file, runs no command and keeps nothing from one call to the next.

## Our conventions

The Knowledge Islands system uses "model" in this sense and has no convention of its own to map onto it.
