# Guild

A guild lets a session find the [skills][industry.skill] it needs among many, by grouping those that belong together and work together under one purpose a reader can name.

A guild is a set of skills led by one lead skill, named after the guild, which says what the guild is for and discloses its other skills as they are needed, the way a skill discloses its own files. It is not a layer above skills: nothing new sits above a skill, and the industry has no word for it, so it is Knowledge Islands' own. Keystone is the guild of guilds: it holds what every other guild rests on and each guild's lead.

## Our conventions

The KI [agent harness's][industry.agent-harness] skills each declare their guild in front matter, `ki-guild`, and a lead the guild it leads, `ki-leads`. The `ki-skills` skill's guilds standard sets the rules, and `ki-keystone` holds the guild map. A guild is kept by its guildmaster, within the backpack a territory carries.
