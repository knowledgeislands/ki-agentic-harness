# Agent (Google)

Google says agent in the industry's sense of an [agent][industry.agent] ([Antigravity overview][g2]), though Gemini CLI never calls itself one ([Gemini CLI docs][g3]).

## Examples

- Antigravity, as an app, the `agy` CLI and IDE extensions ([Antigravity overview][g2]).
- Gemini CLI, replaced by the Antigravity CLI for unpaid and Google One users on 18 June 2026 ([Gemini CLI docs][g3]).
- Jules, read-only after 11 November 2026 and closing on 31 December 2026 ([Jules changelog][g6]).
