## 1. Introduction

A review is the type of meeting that assesses something already made and gives a verdict on each part. It runs by [the meeting standard](standard-meeting.md); this standard adds only what a review produces and how it gets there. No review has been held under it yet, so it is kept to what the type needs. The decisions behind these rules are in [the sources file](sources.md#3-decisions).

## 4. Rules

The rules, part by part.

### 4.1 What a review produces

1. **A verdict on each part** of the thing under review, with its reasons, and, for a part not accepted as it stands, what would change the verdict.
2. **The verdicts are the output.** The caller gathers them in the output folder or the minutes; a verdict is a recommendation, and what follows from it is the owner's decision, recorded in the change document.

[Back to contents](#contents)

### 4.2 The brief

Beyond what every brief holds, a review's says:

1. **What is under review,** named exactly and fixed, such as at a commit, so every verdict is about the same thing.
2. **Its parts,** as the reviewers are to judge them, and what each was meant to do.
3. **What a verdict weighs:** the standard, design or goal the thing is judged against.

[Back to contents](#contents)

### 4.3 Holding a review

1. **Reviewers read; they do not change** the thing under review in the meeting.
2. **Each reviewer gives its own verdicts** before it reads the others', so the first view is independent; where verdicts differ, the minutes give both and the owner decides.
3. **A review closes** when every part has its verdicts, and the caller's closing message names them and the change document they go to.
