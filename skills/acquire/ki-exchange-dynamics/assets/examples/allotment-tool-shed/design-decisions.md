# Design decisions: allotment tool shed

The worked example for the exchange dynamics skill. The exchange is invented, as Kris asked on 4 November 2026: "call them random names". Nothing in it is drawn from a real conversation.

## Analysis

- Source: [`transcript.md`](transcript.md), twelve messages, complete.
- Analysis: [`analysis.json`](analysis.json), nine events selected from the twelve messages; the emoji and the two short agreements that change nothing were left out, except that Nell's "Fine by me." is cited as evidence for event 9.
- What it shows: event 3 is polite in form yet raises tension, and event 5 is anxious in tone yet lowers it, the case that keeping sentiment and tension apart exists to show. The strimmer stays an unconfirmed detail, and Tobias's possible motive is marked as inference.
