# KI-internal trade connections

This delivery implements the user's instruction to make useful connections inside Knowledge Islands without creating formal export routes between Knowledge Islands, HNR, legal, personal or other company groups. It does not activate the previously proposed HNR-to-KI bridge. This is an outgoing implementation handoff, not a new agreement schema or a work queue.

## Implemented scope

Twenty-three registered `knowledgeislands/*` repositories participate. Their individual `.ki.toml` declarations remain the source of truth; registration or Agora membership alone grants no consent.

- Each of the 22 other KI repositories can submit itemized work and knowledge to `ki-agentic-harness`, and receive itemized knowledge from it. This closes the internal maintenance-feedback gap without giving every repository a work-delivery route to every other repository.
- `apps-observatory` and `mcp-acquire-whatsapp` now declare and resolve `ki-trades`, with its canonical inbound and outbound README scaffolds.
- The reciprocal work and knowledge declarations between `tools-techne` and `ki-techne-harness` are complete. Techne Principal's exports to `tools-techne` now match the CLI's existing imports.
- Thirty directional knowledge flows have reciprocal standing consent: 22 maintenance-feedback flows into the KI harness, two Techne architecture-context flows, two Techne interface-compatibility flows, and four existing source-to-website flows.
- Both CEO restart prompts now carry the no-cross-company-route instruction. Wren's prompt explicitly supersedes the earlier bridge proposal.

## Scoped standing intake

These use the existing `ki-trades` receiver-owned subtype model, not a new generic agreement format.

- `shared-capability-maintenance` permits non-sensitive evidence about receiver-owned KI skills and governance. It excludes acquired user data, business content and credentials. The receiver is `ki-agentic-harness`.
- `techne-architecture-context` permits approved Techne roles, architectural constraints and decision criteria. The receivers are `ki-techne-harness` and `tools-techne`; the sender is Techne Principal.
- `techne-interface-compatibility` permits committed interface and compatibility evidence between the Techne Harness and CLI. It excludes credentials and private runtime state.
- `public-source-change` permits already-public, committed source changes for website provenance review. It excludes unpublished and confidential material. Existing senders are Arcadia Principal, KI Agentic Harness, KI Specifications and Techne Principal.

Standing intake is receiver-local and requires the protocol's exact source-commit provenance. It grants no execution, priority, acceptance, publication or deployment authority. Itemized work remains subject to the receiver's own review and work lifecycle. No trade payload was created, transferred, received, released or pruned in this configuration pass.
